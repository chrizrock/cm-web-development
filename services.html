What you get

How I work

Five steps, every project.

The same process whether it's a full rebuild, a new build, or a same-day WordPress fix.

  1. 01

    Audit

    Review the live site (or the mockup) and flag exactly what's costing conversions — cluttered navigation, a hero that isn't selling, a layout that breaks on a phone.

  2. 02

    Design

    Rebuild the hierarchy around the brand and content that's already there — real product photography and copy, not stock filler stacked on top.

  3. 03

    Build

    Hand-code semantic, responsive HTML/CSS — or work inside WordPress, PinnacleCart, or Shopify — with no page-builder bloat and no framework overhead.

  4. 04

    QA

    Cross-browser, multi-resolution testing, checked against pixel-parity so what ships matches the design exactly, not approximately.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Hosting, domains, DNS, and email set up as part of the build; live stores migrated without losing catalog, customers, or traffic.
